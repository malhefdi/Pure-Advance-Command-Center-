# CRM Module — Pure Advance Command Center

## Goal
Add a Partners & CRM module to track current partners, prospective partners, and entities Mohammed needs to reach out to.

## Data Model
- **Partner**: id, name, type (current | prospect | outreach), category (investor | manufacturer | academic | government | supplier | client), status (active | pending | dormant | lost), contactName, contactEmail, contactPhone, organization, notes, tags[], lastContactDate, nextFollowUp, dealValue, products[], createdAt
- **Interaction**: id, partnerId, type (call | email | meeting | whatsapp | document), date, summary, followUpDate

## Seed Data (real PA partners)
- **Current**: Delta (manufacturing), Wadi Jeddah (investment/KAU), SAFEA (education MoU), ACCMi (Coolvex mfg), SAIP (patent office)
- **Prospect**: Prof. Chouayekh (academic advisor), KACST (government funding), NUPCO (procurement), Monsha'at (SME support)
- **Outreach**: SABIC (industrial partnership), KAUST (research collab), Aramco (agri-tech fund)

## UI Design
- Sidebar: "Partners" nav item between "Pipeline" and "Ownership"
- Main view: filterable table (All | Current | Prospects | Outreach)
- Click partner → slide-over detail panel with interaction timeline
- Status badges: green=active, yellow=pending, gray=dormant, red=lost
- Category badges: color-coded by type
- "Last contacted" relative time + "Next follow-up" date
- Add new partner button → modal form
