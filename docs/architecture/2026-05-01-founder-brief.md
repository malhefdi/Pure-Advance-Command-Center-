# 2026-05-01 Founder Brief

## What this product really is
It should be your **operating system for execution certainty**: one place where capital risk, product readiness, team accountability, and partner commitments are continuously translated into owned actions.

## What it is not
It is not a static KPI wall, not a BI vanity dashboard, and not an autonomous decision-maker.

## What is dangerous about the current state
The current prototype blends real-looking UI with mixed-trust data and lightweight permissions. That can create false confidence: people may act on advisory/simulated signals as if they were authoritative.

## Best opportunity
Your edge is not generic dashboards; it is tight coupling of:
1) product lifecycle gates,
2) cash/external commitment pressure,
3) explicit owner/escalation accountability.
This is rare in off-the-shelf tools and can become your execution moat.

## Next build sequence
1. Ship trust contract (provenance + freshness + confidence) and policyed write paths.
2. Make tasks/escalations and invoice approvals real with audit-grade workflows.
3. Add product readiness evidence gates tied to partner and regulatory commitments.
4. Layer AI as read-only advisor with citations after data trust is proven.

## Where overbuilding would be a mistake
- Early microservices split.
- Fancy AI before source-of-truth quality.
- Broad module expansion without enforcing domain boundaries and approval controls.

## Top 7 conclusions for operator decision-making
1. Keep modular monolith; split by domain internally before infrastructure splits.
2. Treat trust metadata as first-class product surface, not backend detail.
3. Finance and escalation workflows are the first production-critical write surfaces.
4. Dashboard should orchestrate actions, not execute highest-risk operations directly.
5. Product readiness must become evidence-backed state machine, not static status labels.
6. Integration health is an executive concern and should appear on the main board.
7. AI can be a force multiplier only after auditability and provenance are institutionalized.

## What should be true before this system is trusted daily by a CEO
- You can explain every critical number’s source and freshness in under 30 seconds.
- You can trace every important action to who approved it and why.
- No red alert can exist without owner + SLA + escalation path.
- Simulated or stale data cannot accidentally drive irreversible decisions.
